Photo gallery from the three-day state visit of the presidential couple to Sweden, 6–8 May 2025. Their hosts were King Carl XVI Gustaf and Queen Silvia, along with Crown Princess Victoria and Prince Daniel. Minister for Foreign Affairs Þorgerður Katrín Gunnarsdóttir and Minister of Health Alma Möller also took part in the visit’s programme, together with the official delegation and representatives of the business and cultural sectors.
